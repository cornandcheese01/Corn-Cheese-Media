What We Do
HEADLINE
We start with your consumer.
INTRO
We're a data plus advertising company. Every project we take on begins the same way, by understanding your 
consumer before anything else gets built.
Our Process
Step One: We Look At The Data
We start by getting into real data and real behaviour, not internal assumptions about who your customer is or what 
they want.
Step Two: We Find What Matters
From that data we pull out the pattern that actually explains why people buy your product, or don't. That becomes 
the one truth everything else is built on.
Step Three: We Build The Strategy
AI helps us move through this fast, but every strategic call on your brand is made by our team, not a machine.
What This Looks Like In Practice
This process runs behind everything we do for a client. That includes brand strategy and identity, always on 
social, paid influencer and performance campaigns, and the website that has to carry all of it. We don't split the 
brand side from the growth side. One team runs both, so nothing gets built apart from everything else.

===== PAGE 5 =====
PAGE 3: WHO WE ARE
Who We Are
HEADLINE
Why we're called Corn & Cheese Media.
Corn is raw, pulled straight from the ground. It's the real, unfiltered evidence of how people actually think, choose 
and behave.
Cheese is crafted using that same raw material, transformed through time and skill into something richer than 
where it started. It's strategy, design and creative judgement.
On their own, both are ordinary. Put together, they become something people can't leave alone. That's the only 
kind of brand building we do. Insight and craft, fused into one process, not split across two departments.
A Note, Directly From Us
We started Corn & Cheese Media because we kept watching good businesses hire marketing before they'd earned 
the right to be understood.
Every founder we respect makes decisions on evidence: unit economics, retention, customer calls. Then hands 
their brand to an agency that works entirely on instinct. We built the version of this company that treats brand 
building with the same rigor: research first, strategy second, creativity in service of both.
AI gives us the speed to do this without the old cost or timeline. It has never been an excuse to skip the thinking.
The Founding Team, Corn & Cheese Media

PAGE 4: SOLUTIONS
Solutions
HEADLINE
Solutions built for you.
Social Media Optimization
Your profiles, tuned to actually earn reach, not just post on schedule. We fix what's stopping organic growth: 
positioning on the profile itself, content rhythm and the engagement patterns platforms reward.
Influencer Marketing
Creator partnerships matched to your category and audience, run both paid and barter. We pick creators whose 
audience overlaps with your buyer, not whoever has the biggest following.
Performance Marketing
Meta Ads and Google Ads built around a conversion goal, not a vanity metric. Every campaign is structured, 
tested and optimized against what it actually needs to do: leads, sales or sign ups.
Website Development
Fast websites built to convert, that represent the brand properly instead of just hosting information. Built to move 
a visitor toward a decision, not just describe the business to them.
Manufacturing Marketing
Marketing built for how manufacturing businesses actually sell. Long consideration cycles, technical buyers and 
trust built over time. We specialize in this category and understand what actually moves a manufacturing buyer, 
which most agencies built only for consumer brands don't.
Deep Tech Marketing
For founders building on deep tech and AI who need their product to reach the right audience, not just any 
audience. Our team takes the time to understand how the product is actually built, so the marketing goes past the 
surface and speaks credibly to a technical buyer.
