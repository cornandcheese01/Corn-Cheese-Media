# CORN & CHEESE MEDIA — WEBSITE DESIGN PROMPT

Create a premium, modern website for **Corn & Cheese Media**, a creative, advertising and consumer intelligence agency.

Use the **attached Corn & Cheese Media logo** as the official brand identity. Do not recreate, distort, recolor or replace the logo.

Use the **attached Website Content PDF as the single source of truth for all website copy**. Use the exact approved headlines, descriptions, services, brands and contact information from the PDF. Do not invent additional claims, statistics, services or pricing.

## DESIGN DIRECTION

The website should feel like a combination of:

**Apple × Burberry × Schbang**

Apple:

* Minimalism
* Clean layouts
* Sophisticated whitespace
* Product style storytelling
* Smooth cinematic transitions

Burberry:

* Luxury editorial aesthetic
* Fashion inspired typography
* Strong photography
* Premium visual composition
* Restrained color palette

Schbang:

* Bold oversized headlines
* Confident agency communication
* Strong scrolling storytelling
* Creative transitions
* Unexpected but tasteful interactions

Do NOT create a generic digital marketing agency website.

## BRAND COLORS

Use the Corn & Cheese logo as the color reference.

Primary:

* White `#FFFFFF`
* Near Black `#0A0A0A`
* Brand Blue `#1677FF`

Secondary:

* Off White `#F7F8FA`
* Dark Grey `#5F6368`
* Border `#E5E7EB`
* Light Blue `#EAF2FF`

Keep the website primarily **white, black and blue**.

Blue must be used as an accent, not as the dominant background.

Do not use yellow.

## TYPOGRAPHY

Use:

**Montserrat + Karla**

Montserrat:

* Hero headlines
* Section headings
* Navigation
* Buttons
* Large numbers

Karla:

* Paragraphs
* Supporting copy
* Forms
* Small descriptions

Use very large, bold Montserrat typography inspired by premium editorial websites.

## SCROLL EXPERIENCE

The existing scrolling and transition animation is already good.

**Preserve the existing animation style.**

Do not completely redesign the animation system.

Use the same animation language throughout the website:

* Smooth scrolling
* Text reveal
* Image reveal
* Section transitions
* Parallax
* Sticky storytelling
* Horizontal scrolling where appropriate
* Subtle scale transitions
* Smooth page transitions

Animations should feel connected rather than each section having a different animation style.

Keep animations elegant and fast.

## GLASS BUTTON SYSTEM

Use **premium glass buttons** throughout the website for primary interactive CTAs.

Glass buttons should have:

* Semi transparent white background
* `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`
* Very subtle white border
* Soft shadow
* Slight transparency
* Rounded but not overly pill shaped
* Subtle Corn & Cheese blue glow on hover

### Default Glass Button

Background:
`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)`

Border:
`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.12)`

Text:
`#0A0A0A`

Backdrop blur:
`12px`

### Hover State

Background:
`#1677FF`

Text:
`#FFFFFF`

Border:
`#1677FF`

Add a subtle blue glow:

`rgba(22, 119, 255, 0.20)`

### Button Interaction

On hover:

* Button subtly moves toward the cursor
* Background smoothly transitions from transparent glass to Corn & Cheese blue
* Text changes to white
* Cursor expands
* Arrow appears
* Very subtle scale animation

Keep the animation smooth and premium.

### Important

Do NOT use glassmorphism everywhere.

Use glass effects primarily for:

* Hero CTA
* Navigation CTA
* Service links
* Contact CTA
* Important interactive buttons

Cards and content sections should remain mostly clean and minimal.

The glass effect must feel **luxurious, subtle and editorial**, consistent with Apple/Burberry aesthetics rather than a typical neon/glassmorphism landing page.


## LOGO

Use the attached Corn & Cheese Media logo throughout the website.

The logo should be clearly visible on the white background.

Use the black wordmark version where necessary for visibility.

Do not place the white logo on a white background.

## RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Make the entire website responsive.

Desktop:
large editorial compositions and immersive animations.

Tablet:
maintain hierarchy while reducing animation intensity.

Mobile:
clean vertical storytelling, readable typography and lightweight interactions.

Do not sacrifice usability for animation.

## FINAL EXPERIENCE

The final website should feel like a **premium creative intelligence and advertising studio**, not a template.

The first impression should be:

**"This is a serious, modern creative agency."**

The experience should combine:

**Apple's restraint
Burberry's luxury
Schbang's confidence
Corn & Cheese's identity**

Use **typography, photography, whitespace, scrolling and subtle interaction** as the primary design tools.

Avoid excessive 3D, excessive gradients, excessive glassmorphism, generic cards and unnecessary animation.

Make every section feel intentionally art directed.
